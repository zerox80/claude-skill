# Business rules
- Bulk discount: orders of 10 or more units of a line get 10% off that line.
- Totals are in integer cents. Tax is rounded down to the cent, deliberately, per finance policy FIN-12.
- The total refunded on an order may never exceed the amount paid.
- A customer must never be charged twice for one order.
- Metrics are best effort. A metrics failure must never affect an order.
